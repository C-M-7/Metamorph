---

Add simple, concise, single-line comments wherever necessary to explain non-obvious code, important logic, or business rules. Add a single-line comment above every important method describing its purpose. Avoid commenting on self-explanatory code, obvious getters/setters, or every line. Keep comments beginner-friendly, accurate, and minimal. Do not change the code's behavior, structure, or formatting; only add comments where they genuinely improve readability.
